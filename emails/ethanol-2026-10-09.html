Ethanol Production

Production: 1.065 mb/day
Weekly Total: 7.455 mil bbl
Stocks: 23.942 mil bbl
Corn Used: 105.42 vs 107.75 needed

Analysis

Production climbed 1.1% to 1.065 million barrels per day, a weekly total of 7.455 million barrels. Stocks built 199,000 barrels to 23.942 million after three straight weekly draws. Implied demand slipped to 7.256 million barrels, the lightest since the week ending July 10.


That leaves stocks 5.8% above last year and about 2.4 million barrels over the five year average for this week. Corn use of 105.42 million bushels ran under the 107.75 million weekly pace implied by USDA's 5.600 billion bushel estimate for a fourth straight week. Cumulative use of 582.06 million bushels through six weeks of the crop year sits about 64 million behind a straight line pace.

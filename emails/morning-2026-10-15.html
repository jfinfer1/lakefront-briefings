Lakefront Morning Briefing

Corn:

Dec corn down 3/4 overnight and holding 200 day support after a 480 3/4 close, with open interest down 1,791. Rain and cool temperatures across Nebraska, Iowa and the Great Lakes will slow harvest into the weekend, and private analysts cut the EU corn crop to 44.9 million tonnes from 46.2. A Dec corn close below 479 invites more selling, with hedge selling expected on rallies toward 490 to 500.


Soybeans:

Nov beans down 4 overnight at a 6 week low after China's flash purchase of roughly 8 cargoes failed to spark buying. Soybean open interest fell 5,941 and Dec bean oil has fallen below last month's low. A Nov beans close below 1270 is a technical failure with next support at 1250, and September NOPA crush is expected at 203.7 million bushels this afternoon.


Wheat:

Dec Chicago wheat down 1 1/4 and Dec KC wheat down 2 overnight after 5 to 10 inches of rain in central Oklahoma replenished southern Plains soil moisture. Chicago wheat open interest rose 7,792 and Ukraine's October grain exports have reached only 38% of potential. Dec Chicago wheat support near 649 is the next downside target with resistance at 681.


Livestock:

Dec live cattle closed lower for a 3rd straight session at 225.12 with no cash trade reported this week, while boxed beef cutout rose $1.35 to $376.50. Dec live cattle support at 224.60, resistance at 228.10 then 229.10. Dec lean hogs closed at a new contract low of 66.07 as open interest rose more than 3,800, with the lean hog index at 75.94, lowest since Feb 20, pork cutout $79.67 and next support at 63.90.

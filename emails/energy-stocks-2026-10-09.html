EIA Energy Stocks

Crude Oil: 415.6 (down 8.6M wk, down 8.2M yr)
Gasoline: 208.3 (up 3.5M wk, down 10.6M yr)
Distillate: 102.6 (down 2.5M wk, down 14.4M yr)
ULSD: 91.6 (down 2.2M wk, down 15.0M yr)
Refinery Rate: 93.5%

Analysis

Commercial crude drew 8.6 million barrels to 415.6 million, the biggest weekly draw since mid February and the lowest level since the end of July. Imports dropped to 6.0 million barrels a day from 6.8 million and domestic output slipped 300 thousand barrels a day while refiners kept pulling. Gasoline went the other way with a 3.5 million barrel build.


Distillate fell 2.5 million barrels to 102.6 million and sits 14.4 million below a year ago heading into heating season. ULSD at 91.6 million is the lowest since late May. Refiners are running 93.5 percent of capacity against 85.7 percent this week last year, so the deficit is not coming from a lack of runs.

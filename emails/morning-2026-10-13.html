Lakefront Morning Briefing

Today's Reports:

Export Inspections 10:00 AM
Crop Progress 3:00 PM


Corn:

Dec corn down 1 3/4 overnight with open interest down nearly 25,000 contracts as long liquidation continued from Friday's break. The French crop was cut to another historic low of 7.3 million tonnes, but another new contract high in the US dollar caps the demand story. The 200 day near 479 is the line: a convincing close below it targets the August lows at 460.


Soybeans:

Nov beans down 5 1/2 overnight after 4 straight sessions of lower highs. China cut its 2026/27 bean output forecast 2.8%, but Chinese buying has yet to show up. Nov beans are holding the 50 day near 1270: a close below it targets 1250.


Wheat:

Dec Chicago wheat down 3 as heavy rains are forecast from west Texas through Oklahoma and Kansas late week, good for the newly seeded crop. A Russian drone sank a second ship off Bulgaria in a week and Mykolaiv port was hit after Russia rejected Ukraine's latest ceasefire offer. Support near 648, resistance at 679.


Livestock:

Dec live cattle closed near the top of the range for a 5th straight session with a 2nd close above the 100 day, and cutout gained $2.21 to $375.56. Next resistance is the 200 day at 229.10, then 230.32. Dec hogs bounced off Friday's new low close, but the lean hog index fell to 77.61, lowest since February 21: support 66.20, resistance 69.30 to 69.70.

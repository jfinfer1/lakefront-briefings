Corn:

Dec corn down 1 1/4 overnight, holding the 200 day near 479 after closing at 482 3/4. Harvest is 33% done vs 39% average and conditions rose 2 to 56% good to excellent, while a COFCO executive said US corn is not competitive for China. A Dec corn close below 479 opens the August lows at 460, with hedge selling expected on rallies toward 490 to 500.


Soybeans:

Nov beans up 7 overnight on reports China bought 8 to 10 cargoes of US beans this week. Harvest is 46% done vs 50% average with Iowa and Nebraska nearly 30% behind, and EPA plans to reallocate 100% of small refinery exemption volumes, a positive for biofuel demand. Nov beans are holding 50 day support at 1272 but have stalled under 1299: a break below 1270 weakens the outlook.


Wheat:

Dec Chicago wheat down 3 3/4 and Dec KC wheat down 5 1/4 overnight as beneficial rains move across Oklahoma and Kansas. Winter wheat planting is 51% done vs 58% average, and Ukraine's wheat exports are seen capped near 11 million tonnes vs 14 million last year. Dec Chicago wheat support at 648 is the next downside target with resistance at 679, but an EU ban on Russian grain transit would be a bullish catalyst.


Livestock:

Dec live cattle posted a 2nd straight slightly lower close at 226.370, but intraday breaks are not carrying through, with resistance at the 200 day at 229.10 and support at 225.00. Boxed beef cutout down 41 cents to $375.15. Dec lean hogs closed at 68.020 with the lean hog index down to 76.51, lowest since Feb 20, pork cutout down $3.50 to $80.39 and support at 66.20.
